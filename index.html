The Story Behind ShuhariTech

Why I build.

ShuhariTech represents two things I care about deeply: a martial arts concept from Japan (Shu-Ha-Ri) that has always resonated with me, and my love for technology.

I got tired of modern software being packed with cloud subscriptions, intrusive tracking, and bloated interfaces. I wanted to see what happens when you build software that runs 100% on your own machine, respects your privacy by default, and actually feels good to use.

Each project I build is given its own identity and color theme and eventually, its own character persona. I'm not a corporate company with an army of marketers; I'm one person building tools I believe in, learning every step of the way, and sharing all the code openly.

Learn deeply. Build intentionally. Refine continuously. That’s what ShuhariTech is all about...or just making it compile by any means necessary.
