Built for Privacy, Speed, and Focus

Designed so you never have to obsessively refresh your inboxes again.

100% On-Device AI

Runs directly against local Ollama language models on your hardware (127.0.0.1). Your email subjects, snippets, and bodies never touch remote cloud AI servers or train third-party models.

Two-Tier Smart Gating

Tier 1 evaluates lightweight metadata and preview snippets in milliseconds. Ambiguous messages are promoted to Tier 2 for body inspection under strict batch quotas to protect CPU and memory.

Dedicated Findings Board

High-priority emails matching your custom rules are pinned to an actionable Findings dashboard, ensuring interview screening requests and urgent notices never get buried under promotional noise.

Native Keyring Security

Connect via official OAuth 2.0 with PKCE. Passwords are never handled, and refresh tokens live exclusively in your operating system credential vault (Keychain, Windows Credential Manager, or Secret Service).

Lightning-Fast SQLite Storage

Header metadata and sync state are stored in a local, WAL-enabled SQLite database with UUID v7 indexing for instant startup, time-ordered sorting, and snappy offline searching.

Total Data Ownership & Wipe

Includes customizable trash retention purging and a built-in master Wipe All Local Data button that instantly deletes local databases, executes a disk vacuum, and cleans all credentials from the OS keyring.

How Fumiko Works

A simple three-step loop that runs entirely on your machine.

01

Link Your Mailboxes

Authorize Gmail or Outlook through standard browser-based OAuth with PKCE. Use default credentials or provide your own custom Google/Microsoft Client IDs.

02

Define Watch Criteria

Create natural language rules like "Interviews, recruiter outreach, and screening requests", "Urgent client questions", or "Invoices & Receipts".

03

Review Pinned Findings

Fumiko monitors your inboxes in the background, runs local inference, and highlights confirmed matches with confidence ratings directly on your Findings board.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about privacy, performance, and compatibility.

Do I need an expensive GPU or paid AI subscription?

No. Fumiko is designed to run efficiently on standard consumer laptops using lightweight models like llama3.2:1b through Ollama. It runs entirely on CPU or integrated graphics without needing an expensive GPU or any paid monthly subscription.

Can I connect work or university Microsoft accounts?

Personal Microsoft accounts (@outlook.com, @hotmail.com) connect seamlessly. Enterprise work and university Office 365 accounts may require your organization's IT administrator to approve the client ID due to Microsoft's corporate tenant security policies.

Can I connect my Gmail account? Why do I need developer keys?

Yes! However, because Google treats inbox reading as a restricted scope, public one-click sign-ins require Google's formal publisher verification. Until Fumiko completes that process, you simply supply your own free Google Cloud Client ID and Secret in the app settings (we provide a step-by-step 2-minute guide). Once configured, your personal or work Gmail accounts connect directly and run completely locally.

Does Google or Microsoft receive my AI classification prompts?

Never. The application connects directly to Google and Microsoft only to fetch message headers and bodies over encrypted HTTPS. All AI classification prompts execute strictly against your local loopback address (127.0.0.1:11434).
